# The Journal of Young Physicists (JYP)

The Journal of Young Physicists (JYP) is an online, student-led, not-for-profit physics journal, founded in 2020 to popularize physics and foster the growth of young physicists. Students worldwide submit articles across different areas of physics, and every article is reviewed by our volunteer team before publication. JYP is free for authors and free to read.

## Permanent archive

All articles published by JYP are preserved in the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine.

**Archive of all published articles:**
https://archive.org/details/@the_journal_of_young_physicists/web-archive

The archived copy of any JYP article can be accessed by adding `web.archive.org/web/` before its original URL. For example, if an article's URL is `https://www.journalofyoungphysicists.org/post/abc-def`, its archived copy can be accessed at `https://web.archive.org/web/https://www.journalofyoungphysicists.org/post/abc-def`, even if the original website is unavailable.
